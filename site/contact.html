Contact

Three inboxes, three jobs. Pick the right one and you'll get the right human (or bot) faster.

vectlang@proton.me

Official. Press, partnerships, business, commercial licensing, anything that is Vect-the-project rather than Vect-the-tool.

vectlangsupport@proton.me

Support. Bugs, install trouble, how-tos. Send reproducers — see the support page for the template.

vectlang@yahoo.com

Fallback. If Proton is down, bouncing, or unreachable for any reason, write here instead. Monitored the same way.

vectlangcyber@aol.com

Cybersecurity team. Vulnerability reports, abuse, supply-chain concerns. Mark the subject [security] to skip the bot queue.
